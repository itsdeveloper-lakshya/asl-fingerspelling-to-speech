About Us

Welcome to our initiative where technology meets inclusivity! We are a passionate team of innovators working towards bridging the communication gap between the hearing and speech-impaired communities and the wider world by translating American Sign Language (ASL) seamlessly into text and speech.

Our journey began with a simple yet powerful idea: to harness the power of AI to bring voices to the voiceless with dignity and clarity.
